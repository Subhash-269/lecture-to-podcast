Put reference clips for voice-cloning backends here (e.g. `maya.wav`).

- 8 to 15 seconds of one person speaking clearly, no music, little room echo.
- Use only voices you have the right to clone: your own, a consenting
  friend's, or a clip licensed for it. Never clone a real person without
  permission.

`*.wav` files in this folder are git-ignored.
